## 60-90 second trailer

- 0-6s: title and premise.
- 6-18s: contract choice, route guidance, first encounter.
- 18-38s: three fast build-identity beats.
- 38-52s: escalating floor and enemy mix.
- 52-72s: Warden guard, break, stagger, final hit.
- 72-85s: victory result and project card.

Keep cuts short, preserve readable HUD moments, and use only in-game procedural audio unless a separately licensed music track is approved.

## 8-15 minute gameplay cut

Use a single natural run as the source. Remove only dead traversal, loading, and repeated cleanup; preserve every contract/reward choice, at least one readable example of the chosen build behavior, floor transitions, the complete Warden encounter, and the persistent victory result.
